# **App Name**: Elvora Services Enterprises

## Core Features:

- Comprehensive Service Navigation: Hierarchical menu system to guide users through government, banking, AEPS, insurance, investment, business, cyber cafe, and digital marketing service categories. Each service has multiple options that further drill down to the final piece of information, all accessible via intuitive icons, home & back buttons
- Service-Wise Enquiry Forms: Dedicated forms for each service category to facilitate user inquiries. This includes contact information, basic query details, and service interest selection. Upon submission, auto-generated email confirmation will be dispatched, detailing submitted information.
- Career Application Form: Dedicated form to capture professional's interests, qualifications and experience.
- Trust and Compliance Display: Prominent display of compliance adherence and trust-building elements to ensure users recognize Elvora Services Enterprises as a legitimate third-party facilitator.
- Contextual Legal Disclaimers: A tool that assesses the services described on each webpage, and presents the following disclaimer accordingly: 'We are a third-party facilitator. Services are provided after approval from authorized first-party institutions.', as well as 'All services are facilitated strictly through official government portals.' and 'We do not sell insurance. We only assist in connecting customers with authorized insurers.'
- Contact Information Display: Easy access to contact details including phone numbers and email address, displayed consistently throughout the site to build user confidence.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep blue (#1A237E), conveying trust, stability, and authority.
- Background color: Light gray (#F5F5F5), providing a clean and professional backdrop.
- Accent color: Gold (#FFD700) for highlights, calls to action, and to signify value and importance.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif for readability and a modern yet approachable feel.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk', a contemporary sans-serif, that combines nicely with the body font.
- Professional and universally recognizable icons for service categories and options, enhancing navigation and user experience.
- Clean, structured, and spacious layout with clear visual hierarchy to ensure ease of navigation and information access. Use of sections and dividers to clearly delineate different services.
- Subtle transitions and animations on button hovers and page transitions to add a professional polish without being distracting.
